Automatic Speech Recognition Tool

Speak into your microphone and watch your words turn into text in real time.

Ready. Click "Start Recording" to begin.

Words may change slightly while you speak, then settle once a phrase is confirmed. You can also edit the text by hand.

How it works

  1. Your browser asks for permission to use the microphone.
  2. The browser's built-in Web Speech API listens and sends the audio to the browser vendor's speech service.
  3. Partial results appear instantly while you speak, and final results replace them once a phrase is confirmed.
  4. This page keeps listening until you click Stop, and restarts automatically if the browser ends a session.

Works best in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, with an internet connection. Firefox does not support this feature.

Built with Flask, HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
